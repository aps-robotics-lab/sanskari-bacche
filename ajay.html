03 / What we really wanted to say

Strict sometimes.
Unforgettable always.

Thank you for connecting with us so effortlessly. Your Hindi classes were never just about completing a chapter. They were a mixture of discipline, humour, conversations, and little moments that made the classroom feel alive.

You taught us words from books, but you also gave us lessons beyond them.

Sir, years from now we may not remember every answer we wrote in our notebooks, but we will remember the teachers who made those school days feel meaningful. Thank you for being one of them.
